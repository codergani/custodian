Effective Date: September 23, 2026 | Version 1.0
Zero-Knowledge Guaranteed

Custodian ("we", "our", or "us") provides a zero-knowledge encrypted credential vault and project delivery manager application ("Custodian" or the "Service"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete, non-custodial control over your data.

1. Zero-Knowledge Cryptographic Architecture

  • Client-Side Encryption: All sensitive credentials, API keys, passwords, database connections, and environment variables are encrypted and decrypted locally on your client device using AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2 key derivation (100,000 rounds).
  • Zero-Knowledge Guarantee: We do not store, transmit, or have access to your vault master passcode or unencrypted secrets. No one—including Custodian administrators, hosting providers, or database operators—can decrypt or read your vault items.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Identification: When registering, we collect your email address and authentication tokens via Supabase Auth to maintain sessions across devices.
  • Billing Information: Subscriptions on Android are processed directly through Google Play / Samsung Store in-app billing via RevenueCat. Web subscriptions are handled through Lemon Squeezy (Merchant of Record). We never collect or store your payment card numbers.
  • Encrypted Vault Blobs: Our cloud database stores only opaque, encrypted ciphertext payloads, initialization vectors, and key derivation salts.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and synchronize your encrypted vault blobs across your authorized devices.
  • To send essential transactional notifications (such as password recovery links and optional SSL/domain expiration alerts).
  • We never sell, rent, monetize, or inspect your personal data or vault secrets.

4. Account & Data Deletion Rights

You retain full ownership and the right to permanently purge your account and all associated data at any time:

  • Direct In-App Deletion: Open Custodian → Profile & Settings → Click "Delete Account & Data". This permanently and irrevocably deletes your profile, clients, projects, credentials, and encrypted records from our servers.
  • Email Request: You may also request complete data deletion by emailing us at ygpksr456@gmail.com.
