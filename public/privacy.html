Zero-Knowledge Guaranteed

Custodian ("we", "our", or "us") provides a zero-knowledge encrypted credential vault, API key manager, and client delivery platform ("Custodian" or the "Service"). We believe that your private credentials and infrastructure secrets belong only to you and your authorized clients.

1. Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how your privacy is guaranteed by our cryptographic design.

A. Zero-Knowledge Cryptographic Architecture

  • Client-Side Encryption: All sensitive credentials, API keys, passwords, database URLs, and notes are encrypted locally in your web browser or mobile app before sync using AES-256-GCM with 100,000 PBKDF2 derivation rounds.
  • Zero Server Access: Our database stores only encrypted ciphertext strings and cryptographic salts. Custodian operators, engineers, and cloud hosts have mathematically zero access to your plaintext secrets or master passwords.

B. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Email address and username to manage authentication tokens securely via Supabase Auth.
  • Encrypted Vault Data: Opaque, encrypted ciphertext payloads representing your stored clients, projects, and secrets.
  • Billing Data: Subscriptions on Android mobile are processed directly by Google Play / Samsung Store in-app billing via RevenueCat. Web subscriptions are handled through Lemon Squeezy (Merchant of Record). We never collect or store your payment card numbers.

C. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate your session and sync your encrypted blobs across your authorized devices.
  • To send account recovery emails and optional advance renewal watchdog notices.
  • We never sell, rent, monetize, or inspect your personal data or vault secrets.

2. Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Custodian adheres to strict data minimization principles. We do not use third-party tracking, advertising, or cross-site profiling cookies.

  • Zero Advertising Cookies: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies (no Facebook Pixel, no ad trackers).
  • Local Storage (Essential Only): On mobile and web, we use browser localStorage solely to remember essential user preferences (such as light/dark mode theme, sticky note positions, and whether the first-launch welcome screen has been viewed).
  • Session Storage (Security In-Memory): We use browser sessionStorage to hold active, decrypted master keys in memory during an active logged-in session. These keys are purged automatically upon lock, tab closure, or inactivity timeout.
